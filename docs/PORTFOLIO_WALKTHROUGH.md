# Historical Brazil portfolio walkthrough

Start the [isolated demo](DEMO.md). It opens the Brazil portfolio directly; no
international/European demo accounts or legacy household scenario are offered.

Spending now includes 24 months of fictional household purchases and income. Goals
includes Emergency reserve, Vacation, Vehicle upgrade, Design course and Home
studio; the latter four share Savings without double-counting its balance.

1. **Investments:** compare total gross value with invested principal. Select 1M,
   3M, 6M, 1Y or ALL; the chart uses dated observations and application markers.
2. **Holdings / Fixed Income:** inspect the four fictional CDB contracts and Tesouro
   Selic 2029. Review application/maturity, principal, gross interest, estimated
   IR/IOF, liquidity and provenance. Treasury uses official dated unit prices.
3. **Caixinha:** inspect its seven separate RDB application lots. Contributions
   move from Checking through linked transfers, not expenses. Accrual creates no
   daily cash-income postings.
4. **Performance:** compare TWR and annualized XIRR with portfolio-value growth.
   Contributions are external flows, not investment returns.
5. **Portfolio Insights:** inspect cash-inclusive account/security/classification
   breakdowns. Missing ETF coverage is explicit; no substitute holdings are invented.
6. **Net Worth / Goals:** verify that investment value and brokerage cash are counted
   once. The emergency reserve goal reads its reserved Caixinha account's gross
   value without creating an additional asset.
7. **Data and reset:** expand Historical market data and assumptions for source
   links and estimates. Reset demo restores only this isolated portfolio after
   confirmation; personal data and archived demo files stay untouched.

WRLD11/IVVB11 purchases become available only after the
[authorized local snapshot setup](REAL_MARKET_DEMO.md). The current included
observations do not contain real ETF price history. All contract terms and the
investor's transactions remain fictional even when market observations are real.
